# Round 4: Business Pitch Presentation Script & Executive Guide

**Project:** TrustWave Bank Customer Engagement & Service Analytics  
**Use Case:** 08 - Bank Customer Insight  
**Target Audience:** Bank Executive Leadership & Operations Committee  

---

## 🎙️ Executive Pitch Script (3 to 5 Minute Verbal Pitch)

### Slide 1: Introduction & Executive Purpose
> "Good morning members of the board and management team. Today, I am presenting our comprehensive data-driven diagnostic of TrustWave Bank’s customer engagement, channel utilization, and service delivery performance across our 500 customer segment sample. Our objective is clear: understand how customers interact with our services, pinpoint where operational friction and weak engagement exist, and execute targeted strategies to elevate customer experience while reducing operating costs."

---

### Slide 2: Context & Diagnostic Highlights
> "Our diagnostic reveals three critical operational realities:
> 1. Over **70% of all customer touchpoints** now occur through mobile and internet banking, demonstrating that digital is our primary relationship channel.
> 2. However, **52.4% of total customer complaints** are concentrated in just two geographic markets—our **North and South regions**.
> 3. Furthermore, **over 55% of incoming customer service tickets** are repetitive, low-complexity requests—specifically loan status checks, statement requests, and password resets.
>
> This creates an unnecessary operational burden on our branch staff and support call centers, driving up costs and degrading customer satisfaction."

---

### Slide 3: Channel Dynamics & Demographic Shift
> "Examining channel behavior by customer demographics reveals a clear generational pattern:
> - Younger demographics aged **18 to 35** are almost exclusively digital, recording over **14 mobile interactions per month** with near-zero physical branch visits.
> - Meanwhile, senior customers aged **51 and above** maintain the same high digital transaction rate, but also record **1.5 branch visits per month—twice the rate of younger segments**.
>
> Branch visits carry the highest cost per interaction. Rather than forcing branch closures, our opportunity is to provide guided digital onboarding for seniors to migrate routine account tasks to mobile."

---

### Slide 4: Account Activity & Balance Distribution
> "Evaluating product usage across account types shows that **Savings and Checking accounts** remain active daily touchpoints.
> - However, **Current accounts and Fixed Deposits** exhibit flat post-opening transaction velocity, signaling customer dormancy and attrition risk.
> - In terms of balance bands, **75% of our customer base** sits in Low (<$1k) and Medium ($1k–$10k) balance tiers. To expand our deposit base, we must convert Medium balance customers into High balance tiers through automated balance-nurture programs."

---

### Slide 5: Regional Friction & Operational Failure Points
> "When analyzing customer complaints, the **North Region recorded 139 complaints** and the **South Region recorded 123 complaints**.
> - Root-cause analysis identifies **App Crashes** in the North and **Long Wait Times/Staff Behavior** in the South as key drivers.
> - Alarmingly, **15.2% of all complaint logs remain unresolved or incomplete** due to inconsistent record-keeping and delayed escalation SLAs. Unresolved complaints are the single greatest predictor of customer churn."

---

### Slide 6: Strategic Recommendations
> "To address these issues, we propose a 4-point data-driven action plan:
> 1. **Deploy Mobile Self-Service Automation:** Implement an automated chatbot for loan status tracking and self-service password reset, targeting a **40% reduction in support tickets**.
> 2. **Overhaul Regional SLAs:** Establish strict 24-hour resolution SLAs and mandatory complaint logging protocols in North and South branches to achieve **>95% resolution rates**.
> 3. **Launch the 'Digital Assist' Program:** Introduce branch-guided digital literacy workshops for customers aged 51+ to migrate routine physical visits to mobile.
> 4. **Activate Account Reactivation Triggers:** Implement automated engagement campaigns targeting dormant Fixed Deposit and Current account holders."

---

### Slide 7: Expected Business Impact & Roadmap
> "Execution of this roadmap over the next 6 months will deliver:
> - A **35% reduction in ticket processing costs**.
> - An increase in complaint resolution speed across North/South regions to **>95%**.
> - A **25% migration of routine senior branch visits** to digital channels.
>
> Thank you, and I look forward to your questions and approval to move forward with Phase 1 implementation."

---
